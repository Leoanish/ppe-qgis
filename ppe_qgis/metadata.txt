about=Plant Pixel Extractor (PPE) separates plant pixels from soil in drone orthomosaics without training data. It ranks the vegetation indices the image allows by how well they spread plant from soil, splits the three best with four automatic thresholds (Otsu, CDF knee, k-means, multi-Otsu) and lets the twelve results vote. Guards remove shadows, algae on wet soil and grey objects. Works with RGB cameras, multispectral sensors (MicaSense, Sequoia, DJI and others, as one file, a folder or separate band files) and hyperspectral cubes; thermal bands are ignored. The image appears on the map as soon as it is chosen, a live slider previews the mask, and results export as rasters, per-band and per-index layers, and plot statistics for an AOI. No extra installation: it uses the GDAL and NumPy that ship with QGIS. Tested on QGIS 3.34 (Linux); feedback from Windows and macOS users is welcome.

changelog=3.9.0: Several crops in one image. On the Review tab the user adds each crop and draws a rough outline around it on the map (click the corners, right-click to finish); each crop then gets its own threshold slider with its own canopy, the slider above sets the rest of the image, and the live preview shows every area at its own threshold. Finalize writes one mask with each area at its own threshold; the vote itself is unchanged, so segmentation is the same as before. The crop and the threshold used are added to each plot in the plot statistics. Optional information about the field (growth stage, planting and flight dates, soil moisture, weeds, notes) on the Image tab is saved with the results in ppe_run_record.json and the plot CSV files; at flowering or drying down the Review tab gives a threshold tip. The plot export shows its progress on the Plots tab.
    3.8.0: Suggested threshold. After a run the slider starts at a threshold suggested for this image instead of a fixed 0.50: the value whose mask best matches the pixels that are at least half plant, estimated by mixing the image's own pure-plant and pure-soil spectra tile by tile (pale plots are judged against their own plants; pixels that are neither plant nor soil, such as algae and shade, are left out). The Review tab shows the suggestion and its good range, a button returns to it, and the threshold curve marks both. Tested against an independent plant mask (Tifton, whole field IoU 87.1 at 0.50 vs 91.3 suggested), an unmixing reference (IH, 71.5 vs 83.9) and labelled pixels (Kritis, unchanged at 100). Needs a NIR band; camera RGB images start at 0.50 and say why. Segmentation itself is unchanged. Plot statistics: plot files can be a Shapefile (choose the .shp or any of its side files), a zipped Shapefile or GeoPackage, GeoPackage, GeoJSON, KML/KMZ, GML, FlatGeobuf or SpatiaLite; the chosen plots appear on the map as 'PPE · Plots' with their IDs (labels can be switched off, the layer restyled like any QGIS layer) and the tab says how many lie over the image. The export now has canopy cover per plot (plot and plant pixels, canopy percent, plot and plant area in square metres) and, for every band and index, mean, median, SD, CV, min, P10, Q25, Q75, P90 and max of the plant pixels.
    3.7.7: Release clean-up for the QGIS plugin repository. The bright-neutral rejection raster is no longer added to the Layers panel (it stays in the run folder with the other guard diagnostics), so the map holds only the image, the live preview and the applied mask. Unused code removed; errors that were silently ignored are now written to the log. Segmentation unchanged.
    3.7.6: Image type is detected from the bands actually found, not from the band count. A 3-band NIR/red-edge/red file (or folder) is now reported as multispectral, not RGB; the Auto-detect entry names what was found (e.g. 'Auto-detect · Multispectral · 3 bands') and the note lists the bands and how the image is shown. A 3-band file with no band names asks for its band order, which now also offers NIR, red edge, red and NIR, red, green (colour-infrared camera). Segmentation unchanged.
    3.7.5: Robust across sensors, crops and growth stages. (1) Shaded leaves stay plant: the shadow guard now keeps dark pixels whose NDVI (and NDRE, when there is a red-edge band) is at plant level, which matters in dense late-season canopies (tested on Kritis, 8 Aug 2025: shaded leaf kept 76.5 percent -> 99.8 percent, no extra soil). (2) Camera (RGB-only) images get a colour guard: a pixel stays plant only if it is greener than the soil's own colour, so vehicles, tarps and grey residue are no longer called plant; a warning appears when plants and soil have almost the same colour. (3) Band names from MicaSense, Sequoia (GRE/RED/REG/NIR), DJI (MS_G/MS_R/MS_RE/MS_NIR) and wavelength names (e.g. 717nm) are recognised; thermal bands (LWIR, TIR, thermal, temperature) are ignored instead of being mistaken for a reflectance band. Framework unchanged (spread-ranked layers; Otsu, CDF, k-means, multi-Otsu vote).
    3.7.4: Changing the image is safe and clean. (1) Browsing for a new image when one is already chosen, switching the source type, or removing an image asks for confirmation first. (2) A new image starts everything afresh: results, slider, summaries, log, export and plot settings, and the old PPE layers are cleared. (3) A remove button (✕) beside each image path takes the image out of PPE and off the map. (4) The panel follows the map: deleting the PPE image layer in the Layers panel resets PPE; deleting the live-preview layer switches the slider off with a note. (5) The image cannot be changed while PPE is running. Segmentation unchanged.
 3.7.3: Panel layout redone and checked in QGIS 3.34 at three panel widths. Labels sit above their fields; drop-downs, number boxes and tick boxes are fully drawn (no half-drawn arrows or clipped boxes); every section is a card, including Advanced settings and Folders; messages grow with their text instead of being cut off; the run summary is short (details stay in the log); every tab scrolls; the Export tab's progress bar only shows exports. Segmentation unchanged.
 3.7.2: Workflow and appearance. (1) The image is shown on the map as soon as it is chosen, before Run: true colour when red, green and blue exist; false colour (NIR, red, green) when one of them is missing; otherwise the NIR band in grey. The first tab says what is shown and which bands were found. Run keeps the image on the map while it works. (2) The panel's styling now appears inside QGIS; before, it was attached where QGIS ignores it and the panel showed in the plain default look. (3) Images drawn straight from a reflectance file are no longer black (the colour stretch was not being applied).
 3.7.1: Fixes found on a second real field (IH, dense canopy). (1) The all-soil / all-canopy check no longer overrides the mask; in 3.6.0 and 3.7.0 it set the whole IH field to 100 percent canopy. It now only warns, with a stricter rule. (2) Algae guard floor lowered from a third to a quarter of the soil-to-plant red-edge rise, measured from the barest soil; the old floor removed pale plants in sparse plots. (3) The CDF knee is now repeatable: extreme pixels are ignored and, when the curve has two knees of similar strength, both are used with half a vote between them. Before, the same image could give two different thresholds. (4) Thresholds follow local conditions again (Advanced settings; can be switched to one set for the whole field). (5) Optional fifth method, half-mixture, off by default.
 3.7.0: Back to the original PPE framework, with repairs. (1) Layers are ranked by spread again, now measured without units, with one normalised-difference index per spectral group and the top three voting. (2) Each layer is split by Otsu, CDF knee, k-means and multi-Otsu; every layer x method result is one equal vote (12 votes). (3) Algae guard rewritten as one rule: a plant must show NDRE above bare-soil level; on the Tifton test field algae counted as plant fell from 58 percent of labelled algae pixels (3.6.0) to 0 percent. (4) Shadow guard restored to its 3.5.0 behaviour. (5) Removed from 3.6.0: half-mixture threshold, red-edge/red forced as a fourth layer, tiled thresholds. Kept from 3.6.0: all-soil / all-canopy check, single image read, bug fixes, panel layout. (6) SciPy is no longer needed.
 3.6.0: Segmentation accuracy and speed. (1) Only ratio indices vote (NDVI, NDRE, GLI, red-edge/red), so shaded leaves keep their class and results do not depend on reflectance scale. (2) Each index is split by Otsu and by a half-mixture threshold that marks a pixel half covered by plant. (3) Images that are all soil or all canopy are detected instead of being split in two. (4) Shadow guard judges vegetation by NDVI; algae guard uses a scale-free red-edge rule and is skipped without a red-edge band. (5) Fixed: red/green ratio polarity, bed-ridge units, plot statistics on images without a CRS. (6) The image is sampled once instead of three times. (7) Panel layout: all five tabs fit, no sideways scrolling, advanced settings and folders fold away.
